Privacy Policy

Prepared November 8, 2026 · Version 1.0 draft
Pre-release draft: This policy has not yet been reconciled against the final iOS and Android release builds. Confirm the app’s actual SDKs and network behavior before publishing this page or submitting it to an app store.

1. Who we are

JLPT Tamani is a Japanese vocabulary learning app developed by Loopivie, an independent app developer brand. For privacy questions, email loopivie.dev@gmail.com.

2. Study progress and preferences

JLPT Tamani is designed primarily for offline study. The app uses on-device storage for study progress, vocabulary review choices, and preferences. Its core vocabulary and example-sentence content is bundled with the application. Ordinary flashcard study does not require a Loopivie account.

You can reset learning progress from the app’s Settings screen. Removing the app may also remove locally stored study data, depending on your device and backup settings.

3. Information you choose to send us

If you contact support by email, we receive the email address and information included in your message. We use it to respond, investigate issues, and provide support. Avoid sending sensitive personal information or passwords.

4. Advertising and third-party services

At the time this draft was prepared, the development project was reported to contain advertising placeholders rather than active Google AdMob advertisements. Advertising is planned for a future release. This section must be updated before any build with an active advertising SDK is distributed. Ad services may collect identifiers, device information, and usage data and may require additional consent mechanisms and app-store declarations.

The app can link to external websites for vocabulary sources, licenses, and related information. When you open an external website, that website’s own privacy practices apply. Your operating system and app store may also process diagnostic or installation information under their respective policies.

5. Sharing, retention, and deletion

Loopivie does not require a cloud account to use the app’s main study features. Data saved on your device can be managed through the app’s available reset controls. Support emails are handled for customer-support purposes and retained as reasonably needed for that purpose or applicable legal obligations.

To ask about information you sent to support or to request deletion of support correspondence, email loopivie.dev@gmail.com.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when features, advertising, analytics, or data practices change. The updated version and its date will appear here. We will update relevant app-store privacy disclosures as required.

7. Contact
