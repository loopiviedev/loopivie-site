Where is my progress?

Study progress is designed to stay on your device. Check that you’re opening the same app installation, and avoid resetting learning progress unless you intend to start over.

Can I study offline?

JLPT Tamani is designed for offline vocabulary study. External links and any future online advertising need internet access.
