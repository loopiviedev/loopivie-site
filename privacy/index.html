JLPT Tamani

Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

1. Who we are

JLPT Tamani is a Japanese vocabulary learning app developed by Loopivie. For privacy questions, email loopivie.dev@gmail.com.

2. Study data stored on your device

The app stores review ratings and history, chapter and loop progress, active session position, onboarding completion, and study preferences in a local SQLite database. Its vocabulary, example sentences, and kana reference are bundled with the app. Loopivie does not require an account, operate cloud sync, or send your study records to its own server or to Google through app code. Core study features work offline; ads, consent updates, and external links require a connection.

Settings → Reset learning progress clears saved learning progress and review history after confirmation. It keeps study preferences and onboarding completion. This reset does not erase advertising data or consent records held by Google. Uninstalling the app generally removes its local data, subject to device backup and restore settings. Android currently allows operating-system backup, which may copy app data to the backup service configured on your device. iOS device backups may also include app data. You can manage device backups in your operating-system settings.

3. Advertising, consent, and tracking

The iOS and Android apps include Google AdMob to display a banner on the study screen and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable advertising privacy choices. UMP checks consent information when the app opens and shows a consent form when required. The app requests ads only when UMP reports that ad requests are permitted. Where UMP requires an ongoing privacy-options entry point, Settings shows Ad privacy choices so you can review your choice. If an ad cannot load, study continues without it.

Google’s advertising and consent services may process your IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, device or advertising identifiers, ad and app interactions, consent choices, and diagnostic or performance information. This information may be used for ad delivery and personalization when permitted, measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, and legal compliance. Google can receive this information independently of Loopivie’s local study database. The data processed depends on your device, region, choices, and Google’s SDK behavior. See Google’s iOS disclosure, Android disclosure, and Privacy Policy.

On iOS, the app may ask for App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission after the applicable UMP consent flow. If you decline ATT, the app remains usable and ads may still be shown without access to the iOS advertising identifier. You can change ATT permission later in iOS Settings. On Android, the app declares the Advertising ID permission; you can manage advertising-ID controls in Android settings. Consent and tracking choices do not affect access to the vocabulary lessons. For questions about data processed by Google, see Google’s privacy information.

4. Support and external websites

If you email support, Loopivie receives your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it to respond and investigate the issue, and retain correspondence as reasonably needed for support or applicable legal obligations. The app does not automatically attach study history to a support email. To request deletion of support correspondence, email loopivie.dev@gmail.com.

The app links to this policy and to external vocabulary-source and license websites. Opening those links lets the destination process your visit under its own policy. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub says it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security.

5. Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date if the app’s data practices change. App-store privacy disclosures will be reviewed against the final release build.
